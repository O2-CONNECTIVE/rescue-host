## Minimal candidate and local calibration

Official [hyper-util client source](https://github.com/hyperium/hyper-util/blob/d5740116a55cbf7af13d1142b365c56b1d684f3a/src/client/legacy/client.rs#L275-L325) sets captured connection metadata inside try_send_request, after connection_for and before the pooled protocol/request transmission call. [CaptureConnection](https://github.com/hyperium/hyper-util/blob/d5740116a55cbf7af13d1142b365c56b1d684f3a/src/client/legacy/connect/capture.rs#L72-L118) stores an extension/watch channel. Pooled reuse is possible. Our actual downloaded0.1.19 source was checked, not a guessed latest API.

`connection.patch` attaches that extension to the existing native request, retains its handle in the existing guard and snapshots **only Option<bool>** at headers/error/drop. Attachment is separately guarded by recorder presence. The borrow ends before recorder entry; no asynchronous wait_for_connection_metadata, metadata serialization, extra task, polling, request, wire header, timeout or retry is added. Null means capture unavailable/not attached, never false or pass. A distinct proposal variant and field keep old records immutable; ordinary schema1 verifier intentionally rejects the new format. Adoption needs its own strict verifier/pins/workflow review; none is implemented here.

This adds allocation and watch synchronization, including a synchronous watch borrow/RwLock read. It is neither lock-free nor timing-free, even though it adds no async wait or polling. The existing32-event/8192-byte/256-byte-row limits remain. A future no-repro under this probe is inconclusive. A true value means assigned metadata, not OS-connect/request-sent/Edge receipt/WebView readiness.
